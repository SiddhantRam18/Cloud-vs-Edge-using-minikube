# Use of Generative AI (GenAI)

## Purpose of AI Usage
During this coursework, generative AI tools were used strictly in an assistive role for troubleshooting, clarification, and formatting purposes. The primary goal was to support understanding, resolve technical issues, and improve the presentation of written content.

---

## How AI Was Used

The following areas involved AI assistance:

- Interpreting error messages encountered during Kubernetes setup  
- Troubleshooting issues related to Minikube, metrics-server, and Helm installation  
- Clarifying command usage and syntax for tools such as kubectl, ApacheBench, and iperf3  
- Understanding configuration steps for Prometheus and Grafana setup  
- Formatting written content to improve structure, clarity, and readability  

---

## How AI Output Was Applied

All suggestions provided by AI were:

- Carefully reviewed and validated before use  
- Applied selectively to resolve specific technical issues  
- Used to refine the structure and presentation of written content  
- Adapted to reflect personal understanding before inclusion  

No AI-generated content was directly used without understanding or modification.

The following tasks were performed independently:
